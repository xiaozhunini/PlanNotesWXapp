## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted for high-retention personal mastery and intentional living within the WeChat Mini-Program ecosystem. Bridging self-actualization frameworks (OKRs, progressive weekly sprints, mindful daily execution, structured retrospectives) with lightweight mobile interactions, the aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Warm Modernism** and **Tactile Minimalism**.

The personality balances serene clarity with focused momentum:
- **Calm & Non-Judgmental:** Avoids aggressive gamification, shame-inducing red alerts, or visual noise. Progress feels like tending a garden rather than surviving a high-pressure corporate backlog.
- **Clear & Systematic:** High contrast between nested strategic levels (Yearly Vision → Quarterly OKRs → Weekly Sprints → Daily Action Units).
- **Mobile-Native Native Feel:** Custom-tailored to the constraints of the WeChat WebView, respecting safe-area top navigation capsules, thumb-zone mechanics, bottom sheets, and single-handed vertical flick gestures.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional landscape rooted in vitality, clarity, and deliberate reflection:

- **Primary (`#10B981` — Sage Jade):** Used for completed milestones, key action affirmations, ongoing progress bars, and the core brand identity. Evokes organic personal progress and calm accomplishment.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6` — Horizon Azure):** Reserved for forward-looking objectives, focus sessions, active timeblocks, and analytical insights across OKR trees.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` — Warm Amber):** Denotes active focus tags, priority indicators (P1 tasks), streak milestones, and morning/evening reflective prompts.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:** 
  - Canvas Base: `#F8FAFC` (Slate Tinted Snow) provides a clean, eye-resting baseline.
  - Sub-Canvas / Grouped Backing: `#F1F5F9` creates soft compartmentalization behind floating cards.
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) ensures crisp readouts for dense checklist matrices.
- **Neutrals & Text:**
  - Primary Text: `#0F172A` (Deep Slate Slate) for prominent headers and active goals.
  - Secondary Text: `#475569` for descriptions, sub-targets, and timeline notes.
  - Tertiary / Placeholder Text: `#94A3B8` for meta timestamps, deactivated elements, and input placeholding.
  - Structural Border Line: `#E2E8F0` at 0.5px or 1px stroke weight.

## Typography

The type scale is tailored for seamless bilingual harmony between Simplified Chinese (PingFang SC) and Latin alphanumeric tokens (Plus Jakarta Sans for display, Be Vietnam Pro for interface text).

- **Latin Glyphs & Stat Visuals:** Latin digits and metric callouts leverage `Plus Jakarta Sans` for tight, geometric, modern counters and high readability inside tiny pill tags.
- **Ideographic Hierarchy (Chinese Glyphs):** Simplified Chinese glyphs align with system-native rendering (`-apple-system`, `PingFang SC`, `Heiti SC`) to eliminate web-font loading lag within WeChat.
- **Tight Vertical Rhythm:** Mobile viewport verticality mandates condensed vertical line-height ratios (between 1.35 and 1.5) to keep daily schedules visible without excessive page scrolling.

## Layout & Spacing

A compact, 4px/8px-based base unit provides the density necessary to display complex timeblocks and goal dependencies on mobile viewports:

- **WeChat Frame Anchors:** 
  - Standard top inset offsets the Mini-Program custom navigation bar and right-side capsule controls (`88px` reference for iPhone standard safe area).
  - Bottom padding strictly accommodates the iOS home indicator safe area (`34px`).
- **Horizontal Framing:** Full-screen views employ a uniform `16px` gutter (`screen-margin`), maximizing tap targets while avoiding accidental bezel interactions.
- **Information Chunking:** Card elements group daily time-slots and key-result progressions using `12px` inner spacing for multi-item task lists and `16px` for high-level goal overview summaries.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections (e.g., Weekly Plan vs. Today’s Schedule) maintain an `20px` to `24px` separation, preventing list fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Subtle Ambient Shadows** paired with **Tonal Surface Layering**, preserving flat legibility while giving touch targets an inviting, liftable feel:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` — Deepest canvas plane.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Containers / Groups):** `#F1F5F9` with a `1px` inner border of `#E2E8F0` for recessed background groupings (e.g., empty state boxes, inactive daily timeline slots).
- **Level 2 (Cards & Tasks):** `#FFFFFF` surface elevated with an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Accompanied by a delicate perimeter outline: `border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.7)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Sticky Controls, Bottom Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` elevated with high diffusion: `box-shadow: 0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Active State Depth:** Tapping tasks or buttons applies a micro-scale transition (`scale(0.985)`) rather than heavy shadow shifts to preserve smooth 60fps renders on lower-spec Android devices.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a **Rounded Level 2** visual vocabulary, matching the warmth and accessibility typical of modern WeChat experiences:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges):** `8px` (`rounded-md`) ensures clean alignment alongside typographic baselines.
- **Interactive Cards (Daily Tasks, Plan Blocks):** `14px`–`16px` (`rounded-lg`) creates an approachable, soft container shape.
- **Major Enclosures (OKR Dashboards, Bottom Drawers):** `20px`–`24px` (`rounded-xl`) softens large viewport elements.
- **Floating Controls & Category Tags:** Full pill-shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) are exclusively applied to status tags, quick filters, and secondary control buttons.

## Components

### 1. Cards & Goal Containers
- **OKR Hierarchy Card:** White canvas (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` border radius, micro-border (`#E2E8F0`). Header features the Objective title with a rounded progress indicator circle. Key Results are nested inside with light grey divider lines, individual progress percentages in `label-sm`, and an interactive completion swipe gesture.
- **Daily Schedule Time-Block:** Two-column grid inside a card: left-hand column lists time markers (`label-md` in `#64748B`), right-hand container displays the action item with a `3px` left-side color accent strip (Primary `#10B981` for focus work, Secondary `#3B82F6` for operations, Tertiary `#F59E0B` for personal care).

### 2. Checkboxes & Progress Toggles
- **Task Checkboxes:** Circular elements (`22px` diameter) with a `1.5px` border (`#CBD5E1`). When checked, smoothly transition to full `#10B981` fill with a white checkmark icon, triggering a strike-through on the adjacent task text.
- **Micro-Progress Bar:** `6px` track height, `#E2E8F0` track fill, with rounded pill caps. Dynamic fill uses `#10B981` for goals on-track, shifting to `#F59E0B` when targets are at risk.

### 3. Chips & Filter Tags
- **Dimension Filters (Year / Quarter / Week / Day):** Segmented control with a `#F1F5F9` background container and white floating pill selectors.
- **Status Chips:** Pill badges (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal) in tinted soft fills:
  - Sage Jade Tag: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`.
  - Amber Priority Tag: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`.
  - Blue Planning Tag: Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`.

### 4. Buttons & CTA Elements
- **Primary Action Button:** Full-bleed or full-width floating action button (FAB) inside WeChat thumb zone. Height `48px`, background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg`, `rounded-full`, with soft shadow `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Subtle background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`, zero border, `rounded-xl`.
- **WeChat Interaction Button:** Outlined button with WeChat green tint or subtle outline for seamless "Share Plan with Accountability Partner" social actions.

### 5. Input Fields & Retrospective Text Areas
- **Daily Reflection Area:** Minimalist rich-text area on `#FFFFFF`, padded `14px`, rounded `12px`, with placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Minimal bottom bar counts characters and auto-saves progress.
- **Quick-Add Single Line Input:** Direct in-line creation bar anchored above keyboard with an instant category-chip picker.

### 6. Reflection & Review Modules
- **Weekly Rating Bar:** 5-point discrete mood & productivity selector using minimal organic vector icons with label descriptors underneath (`body-sm`).
